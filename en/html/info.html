In my studies in the Master Art Edu­cation at the Bern Academy of the Arts (HKB) with a Minor in Art History at the University of Bern I'm moving between working in, mediating and researching within the arts.


My interests often fall on intersections, be it as an assitant at the interdisciplinary Berner Mittel­alter Zentrum (Bern Center of Medieval Studies), the exploration of abstract concepts of self referentiality or the subjective and collective frames of reference, or more physically during my work with printing techniques and bookbinding, the infrastructure, and the people behind it all at my job in the printing workshop of HKB.
